How it works

When a surgeon asks for time off, CAL counts everyone in each of their groups who is already off (approved or pending) in the same morning or afternoon, plus the new request. No Call does not count.

Yellow The request goes through. The surgeon sees who else is off, and Shannon sees it marked yellow.

Red The request is not sent. The surgeon is asked to talk with the others first, and Shannon gets a red alert. Shannon can still enter it under Days Off.

Leave a box blank for no limit at that color.
